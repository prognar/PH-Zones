# `index.html` — OA Zone Alignment Manager

A single-page tool for **assigning 4,695 stores to OA (Operations Advisor) territory zones** across the US. Each OA lives in an **anchor city** and services the stores in their zone. The tool draws every store on a map colored by zone, loads pre-computed assignments, runs an auto-assign/balance algorithm with hard guardrails, lets you reassign/drag/edit, and surfaces violations (DMA splits, area splits, over/under targets).

Version title: **"OA Zone Alignment Manager v9"** / header shows "OA Zone Alignment v9".

---

## 1. Business model & data hierarchy

Store alignment input columns: `CHAINED_STORE_ID`, `FRANCHISEE`, `DMA`, `REGION`, `AREA`, `CITY`, `STATE`, `LATITUDE`, `LONGITUDE`.

Hierarchy (parent → child):

```
Franchisee
  └─ Region
       └─ Area
            └─ Store
```

- **Store** — one location, uniquely identified by `CHAINED_STORE_ID`, with lat/lon for mapping.
- **Area** — a group of stores (named after a person, e.g. "Brundage, Christina"). **Rule: an Area should stay in ONE zone** (1 OA per Area). Only generic filler areas (`N/A`, `Open`, `South`) may span zones.
- **Region** — a group of areas (e.g. "MIDWEST SOUTH", "MCVAY, ANGELA").
- **Franchisee** — owner of regions (e.g. "THOMAS JORGENSEN").
- **DMA (Designated Marketing Area)** — a geographic separator, **not** part of the alignment hierarchy; used as a hard constraint.
- **OA (Operations Advisor)** — a person based in an anchor city who services all stores in their zone.

## 3. Rules & guardrails (the plan's rules)

1. **Load targets — 50-zone Option 1:** every **junior** zone is sized **90–100 stores**; the three **senior** OAs (Dustin Patton, Danielle Hudson, Derron Spencer) run **70–90 stores** each. In 24-zone mode the targets are 150–250.
2. **1 OA per Area.** The whole Area goes to a single zone; assignment/rebalance move **whole areas**, never fragments. The only area "splits" are generic filler names (`N/A`, `Open`, `South`) — these are expected and not counted. Current state: **0 non-generic area splits** (24-zone plan has 15, mostly unavoidable; our 50-zone Option 1 is tighter).
3. **No Philadelphia zone.** The Philadelphia market is serviced by **Tim King from the New York City zone** (zone 12). Every store in the Philadelphia DMA is in zone 12.
4. **Alaska/Hawaii/Guam map West.** Alaska stores → **Seattle** (11); Hawaii/Guam → **Sacramento** (44). Their stores count toward zone sizes but are not measured by drive distance.
5. **DMA lock (24-zone / interactive):** each OA's "home DMA" (the DMA of the store nearest the OA) is locked — stores in it can't be split to another zone.
   - **ABSOLUTE** (`cfg-lock-dma` checked, default): locked DMAs never split; those stores are immovable.
   - **SOFT / shed-when-over** (unchecked): the OA keeps its home-DMA stores while its zone ≤ max; excess sheds to neighboring zones.
   - Per-OA 🔒/🔓 toggles override individually. Toggling requires **↺ Reset to Initial** to re-run.
6. **Drive cap** `DRIVE_CAP = 200` mi (4 h @ 50 mph) for initial interactive assignment; remote outlier DMAs (Honolulu/Guam/Anchorage/Fairbanks) are **cap-exempt**.
7. **24-mode ground truth is sacred.** `INITIAL_ZONES_24` is never re-derived; interactive Auto-Assign touches 50-mode only.

## 7. How assignments load (mode & Option switching)

- **24 mode:** `storeZone` always loads `INITIAL_ZONES_24` (ground truth). Option toggle swaps 24-zone names only (Original vs Manual).
- **50 mode:** assignments load straight from the pre-computed per-store maps — nothing is re-derived at startup:
  - Option 1 · Geographic → `OPT1_ZONES_50`
  - Option 2 · Performance → `OPTION2_ZONES_50_PERF` (with `OPTION2_ZONES_50` as geographic fallback)
- Zone defs + TBH anchors come from `buildZoneData50()` / `buildTbhInit50()` (Option 1) and `*50Perf` (Option 2), wired through `switchMode()` and `switchConfig()`.
- `switchMode` saves each mode's live state back into `MODES[...]`; mode-specific target min/max/bonus are pushed to the config inputs.

## 8. Interactive Auto-Assign (`runAutoAssign` → `balanceZones`)

Used when re-running alignment in 50-zone Option 2 / after manual edits (Option 1 loads pre-computed). Pipeline:

1. **`findOaDmas`** — each OA's home DMA = DMA of the store nearest the OA.
2. **`lockOaDmas`** — every store in an OA's home DMA force-assigned to that OA (unless lock disabled).
3. **`assignAreas`** — group unassigned stores by Area; the area votes for its nearest zone (crossover limit `max(1, min(5, 25%))`, drive-cap fallback 200 mi, then absolute nearest).
4. **`balanceZones`** — iterative rebalance (default 10 passes; early-stops when a pass makes no moves). All moves are **coherence-constrained**: a store may only move toward a zone whose anchor is among its 8 nearest anchors (rank cache), so rebalancing never jumps stores across the country.
   - **Pass A — over-capacity shed:** zones above max donate farthest-first to the nearest under-capacity local receiver.
   - **Pass B — mean-equalization:** zones above `mean + 2` donate to zones below the mean, receiver search ≤ 400 mi.
   - **Pass C — under-min fill:** under-min zones pull only stores whose natural (nearest) zone is the under-min target itself, within 350 mi.
   - **Final crossover correction:** stores return to their true nearest zone when it has headroom.
   - Every move skips locked-DMA stores and skips moves that would split an Area across more than `AREA_MAX` zones (max 2, preferred 1).

### DMA lock modes
- **ABSOLUTE** (`cfg-lock-dma` checked, default): home DMAs never split.
- **SOFT / shed-when-over** (unchecked): home-DMA stores stay while zone ≤ max; dense DMAs (LA, Dallas, NYC) may split across zones when over. 50-mode loads with SOFT behavior.
- Per-OA 🔒/🔓 toggles + the global checkbox; `isLockedStore` / `isDmaLocked` and the top-bar 🔒 count / DMA-split badge follow the effective rule.

## 9. Map rendering

- **Canvas (`#dot-canvas`)** — store dots colored by zone (unassigned = gray); hover tooltip (id, DMA, city/state, area, franchisee, zone/region/FOP, 🔒).
- **Zone territory overlay** (optional "Zones" toggle) — per-pixel Voronoi colored by nearest assigned store's zone, clipped to state borders, 20% alpha; rendered separately for mainland / Alaska / Hawaii.
- **OA/TBH markers** — draggable circles (OA = solid ring, TBH = dashed ring) with labels; dragging updates lat/lon inputs.
- **SVG (`#state-svg`)** — state fills/borders from the us-atlas topoJSON, kept in sync with pan/zoom.
- **Filtering** — FOP/Region dropdowns and zone/area selection dim non-matching dots; filter-summary pill reports per-zone counts with ⚠ (over max) / ▲ (under min) flags.
